GEOTECHNICAL LAB

A. OBJECTIVES

This laboratory test is performed to determine the relationship between the moisture content and the dry density of a soil for a specified compactive effort.

B. THEORY

The Proctor compaction test is a laboratory method of experimentally determining the optimal moisture content at which a given soil type will become most dense and achieve its maximum dry density. The test is performed to determine the relationship between moisture content and dry density of soils compacted in a specified manner.

The objectives of compaction are to:

  • Increase shear strength
  • Increase stiffness and bearing capacity
  • Decrease voids ratio and permeability
  • Reduce settlement and frost action

C. APPARATUS & MATERIAL

  • Cylindrical metal mould
  • Metal rammer (2.5 kg mass)
  • Measuring cylinder
  • Balance (accurate to 1g)
  • Oven
  • Mixing tools
  • Straight edge
  • Sample containers

D. PROCEDURE

  1. Obtain about 3 kg of air-dry soil sample and pass it through 4.75 mm (No. 4) sieve.
  2. Take about 2.5 kg of soil and add water to give a moisture content of about 5% below optimum.
  3. Mix the soil thoroughly and place it in a sealed container for at least 16 hours.
  4. Determine and record the mass of the mold and base plate (m1).
  5. Place the soil in the mold and compact in three layers with 25 blows per layer.
  6. Each blow should be applied by dropping the rammer from a height of 305 mm.
  7. After compaction, remove the collar and trim excess soil flush with the top.
  8. Determine and record the mass of mold plus compacted soil (m2).
  9. Extrude the specimen and take samples for moisture content determination.
  10. Repeat the procedure for at least 4 more tests with increasing moisture content.
Volume Formula: V = (πD²L) / 4000 cm³
Where D = diameter of mold (cm), L = height of mold (cm)

F. CALCULATION

  • i. Plot experimental points and smooth curve for dry density vs moisture content.
  • ii. Report the maximum dry density and corresponding optimal moisture content.
  • iii. Plot curves for 0% and 5% air voids using the formula:
    ρd = (Gs × ρw × (1 - Ar)) / (1 + w×Gs)
    Where: ρd = dry density, ρw = density of water (1 g/cm³), Ar = air voids ratio, Gs = specific gravity of soil solids, w = moisture content (decimal)
